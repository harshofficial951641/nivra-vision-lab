# NIVRA design archive

This directory preserves the project's existing design sources and deliverables, copied from the surrounding NIVRA workspace on 22 October 2026. These include historical explorations; their inclusion does not mean they are the selected or current design.

- `brand-kit/`: earlier brand rounds, editable sources, boards and exports.
- `brand-kit-fresh/`: fresh brand studies, final-kit exports, tokens and logos.
- `brand-kit-ultimate-concepts/`: additional concepts and refinements.
- `assets/`: pitch illustrations and logo studies.
- `deliverables/`: presentation exports and the UI/UX plan, including screen references.
- `asset-manifest.json`: original relative paths, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes for the copied files, including the historical context document under `docs/project-context/`.

The implemented application is in `src/`, with live design decisions and checks recorded in the repository's `design-research.md` and `design-qa.md`. The September project-context document is historical, not a claim that every proposed capability is implemented.

Local environments, credentials, runtime databases, downloaded training footage, model weights, caches and generated training runs remain outside Git. Model preparation and experiment provenance are documented under `service/`; copying this repository alone does not provide GPU inference or keep the public PC bridge online.
