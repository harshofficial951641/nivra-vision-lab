NIVRA / SIGNAL
Fresh brand kit for school-safety applications.

Nivra-Brand-Overview.png: quick visual overview.
logos/: original outlined SVG and transparent PNG wordmarks; app icon.

SVG wordmarks require no fonts. Fonts themselves are not distributed.
The logo is a vector refinement of the Signal concept, not an exact raster trace.
The icon is square in PNG and rounded in SVG; platform masks may alter corners.
RGB/sRGB delivery. Printer-specific CMYK conversion and trademark clearance are not included.
School applications are design examples, not a live deployment or legal notice approval.
