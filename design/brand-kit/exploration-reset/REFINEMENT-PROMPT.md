# Controlled protected-view experiment

Early logo CONSTRUCTION EXPLORATION, not finished logos. A single perfectly opaque solid-white landscape 3:2 page shows EIGHT black graphic studies in 2 rows of 4, each labelled D1 to D8 in small plain type. No other text. No brand name, no letters inside symbols, no monograms, no palettes, no applications, no mockups.

Explore one specific idea for a surveillance/security brand: A PROTECTED VIEW. One continuous overhanging form shelters an open clear channel below it, so the symbol feels quietly watchful and protective without drawing an eye or camera. A simple distinctive silhouette should be memorable before explanation. The main contour is an asymmetric broad top returning along one side; the underside stays open and the negative space looks outward. This is NOT an enclosing rounded square, generic C, bracket, swoosh, almond eye, pupil, shield, helmet, headset, VR goggles, cap, umbrella, bird or letter. NO pointed futuristic origami. Moderate softened joins, flat areas of black with generous clear openings. Resist literal object drawing: make a compact iconic emblem from a protective relationship.

Controlled exploration of proportions and geometry:
D1 broad low top, one blunt lower return and broad open channel.
D2 upright compact form, one soft shoulder, sharply clear vertical opening, no C-like reading.
D3 upper and lower contours share a single fluid connection at the far side, exit is an asymmetric vertical slit.
D4 strongest one-piece version with a single negative-space notch, no floating parts.
D5 nearly symmetrical cover disrupted by a subtle off-centre opening, no literal iris.
D6 heavier top overhang with a very short lower support; generous breathing space.
D7 the same covering relationship translated into solid elliptical rather than triangular geometry.
D8 reduce to the fewest possible shapes and one unforgettable cut; broad, steady, not fast/aggressive.

Draw eight thoughtfully different construction studies, not 3D objects, geometric diagrams, stock icons or a generic UI icon set. Black flat silhouettes only, bright plain WHITE page fully opaque in every pixel, NO transparency, no vignette, no shadow. They are raw trials and no user approval is implied. Aim for mature, unusual simplicity and purposeful form.
