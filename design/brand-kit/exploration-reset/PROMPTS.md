# Rough exploration prompts

## Sheet A

Create a genuine EARLY LOGO EXPLORATION SHEET, not a brand kit or client presentation. Exactly EIGHT different rough black silhouette studies arranged in 2 rows of 4 on one flat, perfectly WHITE opaque rectangular page, landscape 3:2. Small simple labels only below each: A1 through A8. No NIVRA wordmark or ANY alphabet-derived design, no monograms or hidden initials, no slogans, palettes, applications, typography samples, devices, mockups or decorative heading. These are original rough concepts for a surveillance/security platform, human operators observe developing events and coordinate protective responses. Desired feeling protective, watchful, effective. They are exploratory and can be imperfect; vary topology, silhouette, visual reading and construction substantially. Do not make eight tiny variations of one icon. Use bold black filled forms and clear deliberate white cuts, a few light grey construction traces if useful but not shaded 3D. Each sketch must remain visually simple enough to remember; 1–3 components usually. Mature authored symbol design, not an icon pack, clip art, esports logos or conventional corporate pictograms. Avoid shields, padlocks, shutter icons, obvious eyes pasted in circles, animals, lighthouses, swooshes, brackets, generic stars, flowers, arrows and alphabet letters. No prior design reference is supplied. Plain WHITE canvas #FFFFFF all the way to all edges, all pixels opaque, no black haze, glow, transparency or vignette. The page should resemble a clean scan of an exploratory graphic designer's sketch sheet. NONE of these are final brand assets. TERRITORY A: ATTENTIVE PRESENCE. Explore recognizable human/guardian presence through reduced profile/face planes, not a literal detailed person. A1 single forward-looking profile carved by one cut. A2 two opposing profiles sharing a back. A3 face suggested by an off-centre broad slot. A4 attentive brow joined to one upright cheek. A5 rounded head with a deliberate planar facet. A6 two face planes creating a clear open sight channel. A7 compact front/side double reading. A8 asymmetric mask-like plane with neutral expression. Eyes may emerge through negative space but never draw an almond eye with a round pupil as the logo. No angry faces, theatrical masks, gamer helmets or bird heads. Keep silhouettes distinct, thoughtful, not eight identical profiles. Aim at quiet attention with a strong memorable outline. No line illustrations or anatomical detail.
