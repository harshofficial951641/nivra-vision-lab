# NIVRA — exploration reset

## Authority and current scope

Harsh rejected the move from loose subjects into finished-looking brand kits: "I said to explore". This phase is research and rough symbol exploration, not another full-kit round. No logo, subject or palette is approved. Work remains with this assistant; no sub-agent or new task is created.

Retain: protective, surveillance/watchful, effective; standalone symbol; no letter tricks; recognizable forms preferred but abstract possible; eye optional only if natural. Morrow conveys cleanliness and quality, not shape, font, mascot, layout or palette. Lynx, beacon and shell were speculative choices, never selected by Harsh.

The invoked design-preflight skill supplies research, hypothesis, critique and approval discipline. Its implementation contract is for software; the actual artifact is static brand exploration. Professional-logo-design provides the applicable staged logo workflow. No code implementation, application template, palette token or final archive is needed in this phase.

## What failed

1. Premature convergence: one random object became a whole board before alternatives were tested.
2. Category substitution: a neat eye, animal or lighthouse was treated as sufficient identity.
3. Presentation camouflage: mockups, type samples and swatches made weak marks look finished.
4. Conflating cleanup with critique: fixing spelling, opacity or contrast did not make the creative idea stronger.
5. Asking the user to select among unconvincing work transferred creative judgement back to him.

## New primary research

### SimpliSafe / Pentagram

Source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/simplisafe

Read the studio's case study and inspected the live identity presentation. Pentagram describes differentiation through people plus technology, with a coherent visual system addressing a visually similar security category. Observed yellow/navy and broad rounded type. This is useful for the relationship between product truth and a consistent system; its wordmark approach and colours are NOT a NIVRA direction. Do not borrow them or repeat the case study's claims as verified performance.

### Skiff / Pentagram

Source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/skiff

Read the studio's case study and inspected the rotating striped symbol and static lockup. Pentagram ties the form's hiding/revealing behavior to privacy control. Useful principle: a visual operation can embody a product idea and extend beyond a single pictogram. Do NOT copy the striped ring, motion or palette. This is a historical design case study, not a recommendation of the current product.

## Inference for NIVRA

The form needs a specific visual relationship: attention directed somewhere; a view maintained while something is protected; or a developing event interrupted before contact. These are hypotheses, not proven brand perceptions. A long explanation must not be required to enjoy or recognize the resulting silhouette.

Three sketch territories: attentive presence, protected sight, intervention before contact. Eight rough constructions each, generated separately by territory. No wordmarks, colours, app icons, device mockups, or final-kit framing. Black on white here isolates form; it is not a proposed colourless brand palette.

## Critique gate

Judge shape on its own: visible distinctive feature; strong silhouette; no letters or borrowed style; relation to attention/protection; no obvious stock icon; survives visually without elaborate explanation. Critique roughs as roughs, recording failures and the limited details worth carrying. Only after that should three refined concepts be developed for the user's standing selection gate. Do not ask Harsh to select a final identity from these early sketches.
