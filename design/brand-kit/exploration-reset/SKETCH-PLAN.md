# 24 rough construction hypotheses

Not 24 approved or fully developed logos. Intended sketches, judged after rendering.

## A / Attentive presence

A1 single forward-looking profile made from one cut; A2 two opposing looks sharing a back; A3 a face suggested by an off-centre slot; A4 an attentive brow joined to an upright cheek; A5 a rounded head with a deliberate planar facet; A6 two face planes leaving an open sight channel; A7 a compact front/side double reading; A8 one asymmetrical mask-like plane without theatrical expression.

Test recognition and watchfulness. Reject angry eyes, gamer masks, human surveillance stereotypes, faces that resemble birds or letters, or dental/beauty clinic profiles.

## B / Protected sight

B1 an open visor with a continuous overhang; B2 a folded protective hood seen from the side; B3 a short canopy wrapping a clear view; B4 a fitted protective collar with one viewing gap; B5 asymmetric opaque/clear planes; B6 an overhang and underlying aperture sharing one contour; B7 a clipped dome with a sheltered opening; B8 protective envelope with a blunt open edge.

Test whether physical shelter and observation can form one gesture. Reject helmets with military associations, mushroom/umbrella clip art, shields, shells from round 11, bracket marks or generic C shapes.

## C / Intervention before contact

C1 one path caught by a crossing form before collision; C2 reciprocal shapes with a deliberate held gap; C3 a short rising plane that arrests forward motion; C4 an offset mass deflecting a continuous flow; C5 a compressed gesture that opens into a safe space; C6 a stable spine and one returning contour; C7 two opposing masses with a protected central interval; C8 a decisive continuous form stopping just short of closure.

Test an effective protective relationship rather than random geometry. Reject arrows, links, checkmarks, generic rings, play buttons, stock waves and shapes that need a paragraph to mean something.
