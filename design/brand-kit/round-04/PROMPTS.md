## 1

Create a full-color professional brand identity concept board for NIVRA, an emerging human-supervised early-intervention system for public spaces. Product line exactly "Before impact." Brand character from the actual brief: quiet public infrastructure, calm capability, readiness, clarity, human judgement. Contemporary and memorable to younger adults, credible to venue/campus staff. Product observes movement, operator reviews, people respond. NO crime prediction/guaranteed prevention claims. User rejected previous generic abstract app icons, vague curving blobs, tri-part recycling-like shapes, pastel lilac/apricot wellness aesthetics, old warm editorial/Claude style, vintage blue Office look. This needs a recognizable identity rooted in the NAME, with typographic craft. No shields, eyes, lenses, cameras, shutters, birds, police, crosshairs, weapons, AI stars, origami, fake metrics. Do not mimic competitor logos. Full opaque background at EVERY pixel; no transparency or alpha. High-resolution landscape board, refined visual hierarchy with large real logo specimen, a small one-color specimen, 4 labelled palette chips, typography specimen, a restrained public-space sign, a staff credential and phone/app-icon application, plus one branded cover. Applications labelled CONCEPT in a subtle caption. Repeat the exact same lettering/mark consistently throughout. Minimal perfectly legible text. Realistic materials only in application panels; logo and palette panels must use flat uniform color. No gradients, metallic logo effects, haze or ornamental construction diagrams. No explanatory paragraphs.
DIRECTION 01 — THE NAME AS THE MARK. Build an authored uppercase NIVRA wordmark as the whole primary logo; no independent symbol beside it. Strong, precisely spaced medium-bold letterforms, slightly condensed width, upright stance. A shared carefully judged diagonal angle links N, V, R's leg and A, with one distinctive short horizontal interruption in the A crossbar — restrained enough to read instantly NIVRA. I must be present and distinct, A still reads A. N vertical stems, V interior, rounded R bowl all robust and optically balanced. This is industrial design craft, not esports/stencil/cyberpunk typography. The N extracted directly from the wordmark becomes the small icon. Palette: Obsidian #171B1D, cool off-white #F2F5F3, mineral grey #A6B1AE, signal yellow #E9D85B. Yellow used selectively for a thin interval graphic and one ID panel. Main identity large black on off-white; large cropped dark sign photo on right, daylight campus corridor. Mark feels steady and prepared, with measured spatial rhythm. Supporting sans type direction IBM Plex Sans. Type specimen "Quiet until it matters." Four labelled hex swatches. Small phone screen only "NIVRA Respond", "Response requested", "Acknowledge", caption "CONCEPT". Make this a coherent distinctive brand world with mostly clean light fields and one large dark application, not a dashboard or generic card grid.
