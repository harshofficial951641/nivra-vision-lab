# Visual review

All three concepts were independently generated, then directly visually inspected after a background repair. Initial files had incorrect alpha and appeared dark/hazy. Repair used Image Gen, preserving the symbols and layouts on opaque white. No manual raster editing was used.

## Findings

- Alert Lynx: recognizable animal silhouette, plain name treatment, strong citron field, consistent symbol through the board. Most specific pictorial personality of the set. Ear tuft tips and leg cutouts are fine details: not yet proven at favicon size. Animal association may also suggest pet/outdoor brands; security fit remains a user judgement.
- Beacon: clear tower and directional beam, economical shapes, warm orange/plum contrast. Shape survives a monochrome specimen. Familiar lighthouse subject is a risk; do not describe it as uniquely ownable or unambiguously security-specific.
- Mantle: three fitted curved plates, clear separation, compact app tile and one-colour specimen. Abstract form is less semantically immediate and may read as a generic curved technology mark. Included as a comparison, not a claim that it resolves prior blandness concerns.

There is no letter-based invention in these identities. NIVRA text remains a conventional label. Board specimens are raster approximations, not exact fonts or identical vector masters. Small tonal variations in generated swatches do not define gradients for final assets.

Intended colour values follow readable final board labels. Two values shifted during generation: lynx ink is #182619 and Mantle violet is #613BFD. These are reflected consistently in manifest/tokens rather than silently claiming the original prompt colours were rendered exactly.

package.py checks image decoding, dimensions, full opacity, SHA256 evidence, selected text-pair contrast >=4.5:1, archive integrity and presence of exactly three boards. This does not verify every raster label's contrast, trademark uniqueness, final small-size behavior, font licensing or printing. Those remain final-production work after selection.
