# NIVRA / standalone symbol concept kits

Status: superseded. Harsh rejected premature kit development and asked for genuine exploration. No subject, mark or palette in this folder is approved. Current research and rough studies live in ../exploration-reset/.

1. Alert Lynx: recognizable watchful animal, citron and deep ink.
2. Beacon: recognizable observation/signalling subject, orange and plum.
3. Mantle: abstract covering of three plates, violet and lilac.

All three keep NIVRA as plain supporting text, without monograms or letter tricks. Each concept includes the inspected brand board, palette and type direction, app-icon and cover examples, guidelines and CSS/JSON colour tokens. The recognizable routes respond to the user's stated preference; the abstract route remains a comparison.

Initial image outputs suffered transparency artifacts. The final boards were regenerated with solid white backgrounds and inspected. Failed first exports are not included in this package. The latest rendered ink/violet labels are reflected in the tokens; use these values rather than attempting to sample generated artwork.

Final vector master geometry, small-size tests, licensed font files, transparent logo exports, reusable templates and the PDF brand guide depend on selection. No trademark clearance or working product is claimed.
