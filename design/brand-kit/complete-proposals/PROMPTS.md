# Independent complete identity concepts

## 1

Design a COMPLETE PREMIUM BRAND IDENTITY KIT for NIVRA, a surveillance/security technology company for human-reviewed camera events and coordinated response. This is a polished professional identity presentation for a demanding client, NOT a sheet of sketches, not an icon grid, not a website design. It must show a coherent usable visual world with strong colour and restrained elegance.

NONNEGOTIABLE: symbol is standalone, NOT made from N, NV or any alphabet letter. Write NIVRA in PLAIN well-spaced conventional sans lettering, with all five letters clearly readable. No custom wordmark trick or hidden initial. No Morrow style, chunky rounded lowercase font, mascots, animals, human faces, helmets, caps, lighthouses, shields, padlocks, generic swooshes, target crosshairs, camera shutters or gaming emblems. The brand should feel protective, perceptive and effective. A visual/sight motif can occur naturally but no stock almond-eye outline with dot. Use subtle originality and excellent proportion, not an overcomplicated invention.

FORMAT: wide landscape 3:2 fully opaque printed brand presentation on an ordinary solid background, all pixels alpha255, NO transparency/haze. An art-directed asymmetric editorial arrangement with 6 substantial panels, precise 12px gutters and generous internal spacing: (1) dominant logo lockup panel with one strong original symbol and plain NIVRA; (2) vivid campaign poster "Before impact."; (3) four exact colour chips; (4) readable supporting sans type sample "Notice. Review. Respond."; (5) useful physical touchpoint, an operator credential or presentation folder carrying the same identity; (6) a minimal NIVRA Command/Respond digital event card using text "Human review required" and "Review event", plus square symbol-only app icon. Merge panels where elegant; avoid nine equal cards. Include a small mono/reverse logo proof in a logical place. Hero and campaign should take 60% of area. No extraneous invented sentences, no lorem ipsum, no tiny fake dashboard, no gradients in the logo. At most one abstract photographic architectural crop for atmosphere, no stock people or threats. Consistent exact SAME symbol in all applications. Strong large fields of colour, not bland white with a token little swatch strip. Keep logo readable and generous empty space. Deliver design-studio quality with a coherent graphic motif derived from the mark, carried onto cover/card/credential. Small header "NIVRA / IDENTITY PROPOSAL" and tiny concept number.
DIRECTION 01: INSIGHT / electric blue + lime. Main mood: clear, confident, contemporary surveillance technology, technical precision with a human sense of reassurance. Palette exact: royal electric blue #234AE8, bright pale lime #DFF568, ink #111D32, cool white #F4F7FF. Supporting type Manrope-like medium/bold, normal letters. Hero blue panel with light logo; lime poster with ink words; white type/palette panel. The brand symbol is an original compact optical watch-mark: a bold, asymmetric lens-like solid body, with a single clean offset viewing aperture created in negative space. Give it an elegant silhouette with deliberate tension, like a small crafted object whose purpose is to observe and protect. Avoid a standard eye/camera logo, a letterform or enclosing rounded-square app shape. Do not add a pupil unless absolutely necessary; negative space carries observation. Make the form instantly memorable and articulate enough to be a small app symbol. Use its negative-space aperture as a large cut/crop on the poster and credential. NO ray-burst, radar or software-terminal aesthetic. Complete six-panel brand kit, tiny 01.

Carry one enlarged contour from it into a vivid graphic language across poster, pass and simple event card. Complete six-panel brand kit, tiny 03.
