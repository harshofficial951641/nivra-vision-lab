## Assessment

All three preserve readable NIVRA spelling, Before impact., a single principal accent, standalone/reversed mark studies and the human-review message. None uses shields, eyes, camera shutters, police symbols, weapons or suspect imagery. Distinctness comes from mark silhouette, letter treatment and composition as well as color.

Signal Editorial: strongest visual urgency and distance readability; its heavier angular construction is the least gentle. The interrupted N can support a practical one-color identity. Final vector work must retain blunt terminals and avoid any folded/ribbon interpretation.

Civic Cobalt: clearest institutional tone; the threshold can also suggest buildings, so it carries some property/architecture category ambiguity. Its wordmark is more conventional than the other routes. The context explicitly permits this cobalt route despite rejecting earlier generic blue/coral work.

Human Signal: softest, most contemporary character; the compact mark can suggest two connectors, an appropriate handoff relationship but also a possible hardware association. It balances a friendly mark with direct operational language. Black text on chartreuse is deliberate; white/chartreuse pairings should not carry small text.

## Production boundary

These are raster concept boards. Palette hex labels describe intended values; generated pixel colors are not authoritative tokens. Typeface appearance is a direction, not a verified named/licensed font. Marks have visible monochrome/reversal studies, but no claim is made yet about measured 24px legibility, exact curve consistency, embroidery, print color, contrast compliance, trademark availability or vector accuracy.

After selection: refine the selected geometry and lettering, verify small sizes and reversals, set exact color tokens, choose licensed fonts, create logo/icon exports and applications, and render/check a shareable PDF guide.

Approval requirement: the user's standing instructions require three visually inspected concepts and an explicit selection before substantial final visual production. No additional intake is needed; select 01, 02 or 03, or specify a desired revision.
