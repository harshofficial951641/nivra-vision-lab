# Independent concept prompts

## 1

Design ONE original, exceptionally well crafted brand identity concept for NIVRA, a surveillance and security technology company. Its real function: observe camera events, human operator reviews, trained people respond. User's desired character exactly PROTECTIVE, WATCHFUL, EFFECTIVE. The client wants clean and elegant design with meaningful identity. Do not make it cute, chunky, bubbly, retro, sci-fi or decorative. Do NOT use the Morrow reference; no supplied reference image. No shields, camera shutters, stock almond eyes, N stuck inside an eye, police badges, 3D mascots, gradient logos, generic origami N, stock checkmarks, crosshairs, generic arrows or padlocks. An eye is optional only if naturally suggested; do not construct one just because surveillance.

Create a sophisticated flat graphic identity presentation at landscape 3:2. Large generous WHITE or very pale clean canvas. Main upper 65% shows the ORIGINAL primary logo large enough for critical inspection with plenty of clear space, plus a small pure black single-colour specimen separately. Identity is the main content, not a landing page. A SMALL product line "Before impact." and descriptor "Surveillance & response". Do NOT make tagline huge. Lower strip shows four colour swatches with exact hex codes, a typographic sample "Notice. Review. Respond." and two restrained flat application designs: a NIVRA Command event card with "Human review required" and "Review event", and a simple presentation cover with NIVRA and "Before impact." No photographic scenes, phones, guards, cameras, 3D objects, props, fake metrics or gradients. Just beautifully typeset clean flat graphic artifacts. The main logo must read exactly NIVRA or Nivra as specified. Each letter legible; do not drop letters. Maintain same identity in all specimens. Keep sparse hierarchy, thoughtful custom lettering, proportions suitable for a real security company. Rich focused colour in application strip but mark proves itself in black. No invented slogans or extraneous copy. Fully opaque image, no transparency. CONCEPT 01: BOUND. Explore protection as an intelligently ENCLOSED SPACE, with awareness maintained through an opening. Design a compact original symbol from a single continuous broad structural band that encloses a quiet off-centre rectangular negative space and opens with a precise short gap. Slightly asymmetric, firm flattened base, no point, no shield outline. Character of a protective architectural boundary, composed and sturdy. It must NOT look like a chain, generic app rounded square, Q or stock focus corners. Use a distinctive stepped inner edge in the band, suggesting coverage from two directions without multiple loose pieces. Few geometrical decisions, carefully shaped negative space. The symbol is paired HORIZONTALLY with an original uppercase NIVRA wordmark: medium-bold compact lettering, upright stems, disciplined consistent cuts, moderate width and subtle softened joins. No extreme extended tracking, no stencil sci-fi or giant bubbly type. Keep the symbol compact rather than overpowering the name. Palette: deep midnight #152536, confident golden yellow #F5C242, pale mist #E9EFF1, clean white #FAFCFD. Logo in deep midnight with optional gold accent in applications, never golden small text on white. Small text on yellow uses midnight. Exact palette chips. Tiny number 01.
