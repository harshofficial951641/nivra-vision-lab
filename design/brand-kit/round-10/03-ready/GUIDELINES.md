## Composition and language

Use generous clear space, precise alignment, sparse hierarchy and flat colour. Keep the symbol/wordmark primary. The line is Before impact. The descriptor Surveillance & response is proposed, not a changed product name. Incidental generated copy is not approved copy.

Use one identity across NIVRA Command, Camera, Respond, Admin and Trust. The board's event card is an illustrative application, not built software or evidence of real surveillance deployment. Describe human review and coordinated response without guaranteed prevention, crime prediction or identity inference.
