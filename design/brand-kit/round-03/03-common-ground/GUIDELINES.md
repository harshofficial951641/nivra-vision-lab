# NIVRA / Common Ground

Status: full visual identity concept, not approved production artwork.

## Strategic idea
Three complementary parts make one shared opening: observations, human judgement and response.

The mark derives its character from the space between its parts. Rounded geometry and a lowercase wordmark make the system approachable while the dark neutral keeps it grounded. The parts also create a useful pattern for covers and event material.

Character: Human / grounded / clear.

## Color
- Graphite brown: #292327
- Apricot: #F4AA76
- Alabaster: #F8F5F0
- Muted clay: #BA765C

Suggested composition balance: Alabaster 45% / Graphite brown 30% / Apricot 20% / Muted clay 5%. This is a layout starting point, not a mandatory quota.

Use #292327 for body text on #F8F5F0; #F8F5F0 on #292327; and #292327 on #F4AA76. Do not use light text on the accent for small copy. Tokens are authoritative; generated pixels are illustrative. These are sRGB values; CMYK and physical print proofing remain pending.

## Typography
DM Sans: 600 for headings, 400 for body, 700 sparingly for labels. Body 16 px/1.5, captions at least 12 px, headline scale 24/36/48 px. Use sentence case for instructions. The wordmark is custom artwork, not a font substitution. Google Fonts source and OFL licence are recorded in ../sources/font-sources.json and ../sources/fonts/.

## Logo system
Primary lockup, standalone symbol, reversed and single-color treatments are shown on the board. Clear-space starting rule: 0.25 times the symbol width on each side. Final SVG masters and measured minimum sizes follow selection. Do not stretch, mirror, rotate, recolor individual parts arbitrarily, add shadows or reconstruct the wordmark in a nearby font.

The three-part symbol offers the richest pattern language. Its silhouette needs distinctiveness screening because clustered marks can suggest recycling or unrelated community brands.

## Graphic and image system
Repeat the three-part module in controlled crops. Keep the original spacing within each complete mark. Use a single crop on covers; dense patterns belong on secondary surfaces.

Pair fresh apricot with graphite-brown, balanced by alabaster. Keep layouts contemporary with open sans typography; avoid sepia filters and heritage styling.

Photography: Shared campus and venue settings, approachable distance and ordinary people. Fresh daylight; warm materials without a nostalgic filter.

App icon: Graphite-brown tile with an apricot symbol. Use one-color mark for small, low-detail contexts.
