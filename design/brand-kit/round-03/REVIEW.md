## Design tradeoffs

1. Open Moment: two redirected trajectories and an open interval. Thin joins and tips need optical refinement at small sizes.
2. Quiet Pivot: a compact single turning form. Its silhouette needs screening against link/question-mark associations.
3. Common Ground: three complementary forms around a shared opening; the strongest repeatable pattern language. Clustered marks can suggest recycling or community organizations, so distinctiveness needs further screening.
