## What is actually available to NIVRA

The supplied context defines a human-reviewed sequence leading to coordinated field response. It describes a prototype and future roadmap. We can build a brand around that specific journey; we cannot call it an exclusive capability, claim deployment or assert better detection than these vendors.

Proposed position: **A clearer moment for people to act.**

Keep **Before impact.** as the product line. Supporting language should explain observations, human review and response, without predicting intent or guaranteeing prevention.

The visual opportunity is a contemporary public-space identity that can live equally well on a responder's phone and a plain site notice. It should be memorable in one color, readable at distance and human enough to sit in a campus. Distinction should come from construction, typography, composition and the whole application system, not a supposed unused color.

## Reference interpretation

Harsh likes Mannat Group's balance: clean, simple but with enough detail to represent its brand. The actual red monogram was inspected at https://mannatgroup.in/. This is a quality/complexity reference, not authorization to borrow its mark.

All prior NIVRA concepts are rejected. Do not recycle their N/NV marks, heavy editorial type or palettes. The next presentation must show complete color brand-kit concepts, not isolated monochrome logo studies.

## Name and symbol work

NIVRA's N/V/A repeat angular rhythms; leaning on them produced generic monograms in the rejected work. Keep readable full lettering, including an A crossbar. The R supplies a curved counter and useful optical balance. The I can function as a quiet interval in typography but should not become a detached generic exclamation icon.

Explore product-action symbols instead: making space before contact; a trajectory calmly diverted; separate observations coordinated into one response. Avoid shields, eyes, shutters, birds, crosshairs, weapons, sharp origami, surveillance frames, fingerprint patterns, generic AI stars and radial marks resembling observed competitors.
