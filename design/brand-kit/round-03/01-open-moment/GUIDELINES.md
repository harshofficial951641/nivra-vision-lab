# NIVRA / Open Moment

Status: full visual identity concept, not approved production artwork.

## Strategic idea
Two approaching paths bend past each other, leaving room for a different outcome.

The open interval is the identity: NIVRA brings attention to the moment when a person can still decide what happens next. The separate curves extend into a restrained graphic system for architecture, signage and event material.

Character: Composed / alert / fluid.

## Color
- Deep petrol: #113D42
- Sea glass: #59D8C3
- Porcelain: #F4F7F4
- Graphite: #172329

Suggested composition balance: Petrol 45% / Porcelain 35% / Sea glass 15% / Graphite 5%. This is a layout starting point, not a mandatory quota.

Use #172329 for body text on #F4F7F4; #F4F7F4 on #113D42; and #113D42 on #59D8C3. Do not use light text on the accent for small copy. Tokens are authoritative; generated pixels are illustrative. These are sRGB values; CMYK and physical print proofing remain pending.

## Typography
Manrope: 600 for headings, 400 for body, 700 sparingly for labels. Body 16 px/1.5, captions at least 12 px, headline scale 24/36/48 px. Use sentence case for instructions. The wordmark is custom artwork, not a font substitution. Google Fonts source and OFL licence are recorded in ../sources/font-sources.json and ../sources/fonts/.

## Logo system
Primary lockup, standalone symbol, reversed and single-color treatments are shown on the board. Clear-space starting rule: 0.25 times the symbol width on each side. Final SVG masters and measured minimum sizes follow selection. Do not stretch, mirror, rotate, recolor individual parts arbitrarily, add shadows or reconstruct the wordmark in a nearby font.

The open silhouette is expressive, but its thin middle transition needs optical testing before a final small-size master is drawn.

## Graphic and image system
Use one enlarged trajectory crop at a time. Leave the opposing curve or its negative space visible. Keep pattern away from body copy and operational controls.

Anchor with a calm petrol field. Use porcelain for information and turquoise for a deliberate point of attention. Photography should remain naturally lit and recognisably public.

Photography: Open campus paths and public architecture in daylight. Ordinary activity; no reenacted threat or tracking boxes.

App icon: Petrol app tile; porcelain symbol with a sea-glass terminal. Solid one-color version for small icons.

## Product family and language
Use NIVRA Command, NIVRA Camera, NIVRA Respond, NIVRA Admin and NIVRA Trust in prose. Keep one shared symbol; do not create five unrelated sub-brands.

Never claim crime prediction, intent detection, guaranteed prevention or automatic policing. No impact recorded is a recorded workflow outcome, not proof of causation.

## Applications and production boundary
The board shows concept applications of the identity on phones, public-space notices and staff materials. They are generated scenes, not existing deployments or functional product screens. Public notices are visual mockups, not complete operational/privacy signage: the real operator, site policy, retention details and contact route must be supplied for an actual site. No legal-compliance claim is made.
