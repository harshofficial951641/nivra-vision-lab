# NIVRA round 03 image prompts

Tool: built-in Image Gen. Three independent full-kit concept generations; no model/provider switch. Every prompt also required a fully opaque rectangular presentation with no transparency/alpha. The first board was edited to correct the responder message to Response requested and remove filler slogans. Existing logos were not supplied as edit sources; the final first board edit used only its own initial output.

## 1

Create a sophisticated FULL COLOUR BRAND IDENTITY KIT presentation for NIVRA. High-resolution landscape 3:2 or 16:10. Direction 01: OPEN MOMENT. Deliver one coherent identity system in a beautifully composed six-to-eight-panel design-studio board, combining identity, palette, typography, digital use, real-world application and an expressive graphic motif. Opaque image, absolutely solid backgrounds.

STRATEGY: NIVRA is an emerging human-supervised early-intervention system for campuses/events/public venues. Camera observations lead to human review and a responder. Brand feeling: contemporary, attentive, human, composed, quietly ingenious. Exact product line 'Before impact.' Original mark must communicate a small opportunity to act while remaining recognisable at app-icon scale. Invent an elegant compact abstract symbol from the idea of TWO APPROACHING TRAJECTORIES BENDING PAST EACH OTHER to preserve a clear interval. Curved precision, purposeful negative space, a distinctive asymmetrical silhouette with controlled detail. It must stand by itself. Do not force an N or NV monogram. Avoid a circular orbit, infinity, eye, camera, shield, arrow, ribbon, bird or AI sparkle. The final chosen symbol must be repeated EXACTLY throughout the board.

COLOUR: deep petrol #113D42, sea-glass turquoise #59D8C3, porcelain #F4F7F4, graphite #172329. Lead with the dark petrol and turquoise interplay, balance with fresh porcelain. This should feel fresh and desirable, not neon security or a dated blue software company.
LETTERING: 'NIVRA' in a bespoke restrained medium-weight contemporary sans, correctly spelled, proper A crossbar. Character comes from optical spacing and a subtle shared curve with the mark, not giant extra-bold type. Supporting type direction Manrope, readable and confident.

BOARD DESIGN: Make a large left hero identity panel (roughly 40% width) with a crisp flat porcelain mark and NIVRA on deep petrol, 'Before impact.' smaller. Surround it with sharply aligned varied-scale panels: 
1 a clear white logo construction/monochrome panel showing the SAME symbol in flat black and reversed;
2 four generous colour swatches labelled exact hex values;
3 a type specimen 'A moment to act.' with small label 'Manrope';
4 one believable slim responder phone application labelled 'CONCEPT' with NIVRA Respond, 'Human review required', and one 'Acknowledge' control, no fake metrics;
5 one premium matte public-space notice standing on a campus wall labelled 'CONCEPT APPLICATION', NIVRA and exact short copy 'People make the call.' No actual QR or compliance claim;
6 a campaign/image panel of calm modern public architecture, daylight with distant anonymous adults, no danger; typography 'Before impact.' The logo's bent trajectories form a subtle large-scale graphic around the architecture, never tracking boxes around people.
7 a small app icon and a tasteful staff lanyard/credential showing the same symbol.
Use spatial rhythm, rich but disciplined composition, exquisite alignment and breathing room. Several panels can be light; don't default to all black. Minimal text, no paragraph filler. Do not output a website screenshot. No beige editorial, orange/red, heavy poster typography, glassmorphism, fake certification, weapons, surveillance eyes, police symbolism, generic N monogram or fake company metrics. All flat logo panels must be perfectly crisp and uniform, not smoky or shadowed. Lighting only in photographic mockup panels.

## 2

Create one beautiful FULL COLOUR BRAND KIT for NIVRA, direction 02: QUIET PIVOT. High-resolution landscape identity-studio presentation, 3:2 or 16:10. It must show a complete thoughtful logo system, rich colour world, typography and tangible applications. Opaque artwork with clear flat color fields.

NIVRA is a human-supervised early-intervention platform for public spaces. It notices movement sequences; a human reviews; staff respond. Meaning: a developing situation can change direction through a human decision. Invent a memorable compact abstract PIVOT symbol: a broad, softly tapered path takes one deliberate graceful turn around a crisp open notch. Fuse the sense of an interval and a redirected path into ONE coherent silhouette. The central void must be deliberate and visible, not an eye or shield; no letter N/V, no arrowhead, no spiral, no infinity, no sharp origami. Let the design feel like a crafted symbol rather than a common geometric icon. Moderate detail density, simple silhouette with one discovery inside. Repeat exactly the same mark across all applications.

Palette: aubergine #32233F, clear lilac #B9A0EE, cool cloud #F4F1F7, softened ink #201B25. Lilac is a flat confident accent, not purple-blue glowing AI. Main wordmark NIVRA in contemporary uppercase medium weight with precise letter spacing and subtly rounded joins, normal A crossbar. Type direction 'Plus Jakarta Sans', modern and open, no serif, no oversized heavy editorial type.

Compose an asymmetric studio moodboard, NOT a uniform nine-card grid. A broad cloud-white upper-left identity field features large aubergine mark plus NIVRA and small 'Before impact.' A tall right-side aubergine campaign panel includes a rich monochromatic photograph of a quiet public atrium and exact sparse line 'Notice. Review. Respond.' A few meticulously aligned middle/lower panels show: the symbol in black/white; four labeled color chips #32233F #B9A0EE #F4F1F7 #201B25; large type sample 'People decide.' and label 'Plus Jakarta Sans'; a finished lilac staff-pass/lanyard with NIVRA symbol and 'Respond'; a square app icon; a phone corner with 'NIVRA Respond', 'On scene', 'Situation clear' labeled 'CONCEPT'; an elegant event wayfinding/notice application with logo and 'Human review required', labelled 'CONCEPT APPLICATION'. A repeating motif uses the exact symbol's single turning path at larger scale, sophisticated and spare.
Use warm human daylight in the photography, controlled material detail in the pass, exact flat fills for identity specimens. Clean but with enough substance to reward a closer look. No fake graphs, measurements, customer endorsements, deployment claims, QR, awards, intimidating security imagery, cameras, police marks or cloned competitors. Minimal perfectly readable type. Main logo is the focus; mockups support it.

## 3

Create one innovative FULL COLOUR BRAND IDENTITY KIT for NIVRA, direction 03: COMMON GROUND. High-resolution landscape agency presentation, 3:2 or 16:10. One original consistent symbol repeated throughout, integrated colour and typography system, physical and digital brand applications.

Brand: human-supervised early intervention in campuses, venues and events. Observations are reviewed by a person who coordinates field response. The brand belongs in shared spaces and on people's everyday devices. Emotional register: assured, clear, human, contemporary; not luxury retro, police, health charity or enterprise PowerPoint.

Invent a compact original mark based on THREE DIFFERENT PARTS CREATING ONE SHARED OPEN SPACE. It represents observation, human judgement and response meeting without closing the space. Three broad asymmetrical interdependent forms, precision-shaped with a subtle common tangent or complementary cut, compact squarish silhouette and distinctive central negative space. It should feel like an authored emblem of coordination, not generic connected nodes, a flower, triskelion, AI star, aperture/shutter, eye, shield, clasped hands, letter N, NV, puzzle pieces or globe. Fewer than four parts, excellent one-color reading. Let the central open space carry the meaning rather than adding literal symbols. Every application must use EXACT SAME MARK.

Palette: graphite-brown #292327, warm apricot #F4AA76, soft alabaster #F8F5F0, muted clay #BA765C. Contemporary fresh flat apricot with charcoal, not sepia/rust/coral editorial. Type 'DM Sans' direction: medium/light contemporary sans, sophisticated clear lowercase 'nivra' with normal i dot, restrained open counters, NO bubbly inflated letters. The lowercase brand mark doesn't change product names in copy. Product line exact 'Before impact.'

Layout: distinctive horizontal landscape identity board. Large charcoal hero block on left with apricot symbol, crisp alabaster wordmark and short line. Across right top, a large architectural/campus scene in fresh natural daylight, understated apricot wall-mounted NIVRA public notice visible, label 'CONCEPT APPLICATION'. Middle-row panels: exact monochrome symbol study on alabaster; four clean color swatches labelled #292327 #F4AA76 #F8F5F0 #BA765C; typographic specimen 'Shared spaces.' / 'Human decisions.' with small 'DM Sans'. Bottom row uses a wide beautifully art-directed staff credential and lanyard mockup with exact logo, a phone application 'NIVRA Respond' / 'Acknowledge' / 'CONCEPT', app-icon treatment, and a graphic pattern derived only from the shared-space geometry. Show a calm presentation cover 'NIVRA' / 'Before impact.' as a small flat application. Distinct scale/rhythm, clean gutters, plenty of breathing room, premium and practical.

Do not make generic equal-size cards or a website. No random decorative icons, fake legal notices, QR, metrics, business claims, badges, awards, weapon/incident images. No vintage serif, giant heavy grotesk lettering, cream/red Claude look, old blue Office look, chartreuse blobs, N monograms, stock corporate handshake photography, gradients/glows or dark haze. Crisp solid opaque color fields, realistic lighting only within mockup/photograph panels.
