# NIVRA / Quiet Pivot

Status: full visual identity concept, not approved production artwork.

## Strategic idea
One turning form expresses a developing situation changing course through a human decision.

A compact turning silhouette gives the brand a recognisable mark without relying on a camera, shield or initial. Its open notch becomes a repeatable graphic detail across a staff pass, app icon and public-space application.

Character: Considered / contemporary / assured.

## Color
- Aubergine: #32233F
- Clear lilac: #B9A0EE
- Cool cloud: #F4F1F7
- Softened ink: #201B25

Suggested composition balance: Cool cloud 50% / Aubergine 30% / Lilac 15% / Softened ink 5%. This is a layout starting point, not a mandatory quota.

Use #201B25 for body text on #F4F1F7; #F4F1F7 on #32233F; and #32233F on #B9A0EE. Do not use light text on the accent for small copy. Tokens are authoritative; generated pixels are illustrative. These are sRGB values; CMYK and physical print proofing remain pending.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans: 600 for headings, 400 for body, 700 sparingly for labels. Body 16 px/1.5, captions at least 12 px, headline scale 24/36/48 px. Use sentence case for instructions. The wordmark is custom artwork, not a font substitution. Google Fonts source and OFL licence are recorded in ../sources/font-sources.json and ../sources/fonts/.

## Logo system
Primary lockup, standalone symbol, reversed and single-color treatments are shown on the board. Clear-space starting rule: 0.25 times the symbol width on each side. Final SVG masters and measured minimum sizes follow selection. Do not stretch, mirror, rotate, recolor individual parts arbitrarily, add shadows or reconstruct the wordmark in a nearby font.

The abstract hook has the strongest compact silhouette; its notch needs refinement so the form does not read as a generic link or question mark.

## Graphic and image system
Use the single turn as a large edge crop or directional divider. Avoid repeated loops, glow effects and purple-blue gradients.

Let cloud-white carry the name and most information. Use aubergine for anchoring panels and lilac for selected surfaces or the app tile.

Photography: Calm atriums and venues with people moving naturally. Soft daylight, real materials and a clear sense of shared space.

App icon: Lilac app tile with an aubergine symbol. Keep state badges separate from the brand mark.

## Product family and language
Use NIVRA Command, NIVRA Camera, NIVRA Respond, NIVRA Admin and NIVRA Trust in prose. Keep one shared symbol; do not create five unrelated sub-brands.

## Applications and production boundary
The board shows concept applications of the identity on phones, public-space notices and staff materials. They are generated scenes, not existing deployments or functional product screens. Public notices are visual mockups, not complete operational/privacy signage: the real operator, site policy, retention details and contact route must be supplied for an actual site. No legal-compliance claim is made.
