# Round 06 independent prompts

## 1

Create a sophisticated complete visual BRAND KIT for NIVRA, a SURVEILLANCE / PHYSICAL SECURITY TECHNOLOGY COMPANY. This category is the decisive instruction. It must look credible to a security director procuring intelligent camera monitoring, control-room software and responder coordination. Authoritative, vigilant, precise, technically competent. NOT a campus charity, community campaign, university brand, creative agency, lifestyle app, sportswear brand or children's app. The last explorations failed because they were cheerful campus marketing. Change that at the root: original security-industry emblem, disciplined engineered typography, and real security-operations applications.
Primary brand name exact "NIVRA" uppercase. Descriptor exact "SURVEILLANCE INTELLIGENCE". Product line exact "Before impact." Underlying product uses existing cameras, human review and coordinated response; no face identification, prediction of guilt, police autonomy or guaranteed prevention. Do not invent manufactured camera products: any camera is generic unbranded existing infrastructure, with NIVRA branding on the notice/control-room software nearby.
Generate one full-color landscape 3:2 identity board. Strong color is REQUIRED: substantial saturated primary fields, confidently repeated color across applications, not a grey board with tiny accent lines. Balance this with dark operational materials and white readable areas. Every pixel fully opaque; no transparency. The logo gets the greatest space and must be memorable without an explanatory paragraph. A compact original symbol and bespoke disciplined uppercase lettering with normal readable five letters NIVRA, robust normal A crossbar. Master icon and lettering repeated consistently on every application. Design real restraint and satisfying internal detail rather than stock geometric clip art.
Include a dominant large identity panel; flat black/white logo proof; four large labeled HEX palette swatches; small exact typography direction specimen; a professional surveillance notice mounted beside a GENERIC UNBRANDED camera at an office entrance; embroidered uniform chest or sleeve branding on plain security workwear, NO police insignia; a command-room wall/workstation with ordinary security staff and existing camera feeds, marked CONCEPT; a responder phone branded NIVRA Respond with "Response requested" and "Acknowledge", marked CONCEPT; and a technical proposal cover branded NIVRA / SURVEILLANCE INTELLIGENCE. Sign text only NIVRA, SURVEILLANCE INTELLIGENCE, VIDEO MONITORING, Human-reviewed alerts, and a small CONCEPT caption. No schools/campus scenes, no cheerful student portraits, no staff portraits as hero, no lanyard closeups, no aspirational community slogans, no brighter-tomorrow text, no random extra copy.
No shields, eyes, camera aperture/shutters, birds, crosshairs, police symbols, weapons, origami folds, AI sparkles, orbital swirls or copied competitor icons. Do not reproduce Axis triangles, Verkada checks or Avigilon lettering. No fake certifications, TM, numbers, claims, QR or legal compliance text. Flat master logo colors, no glow or glossy 3D logo. Professional surveillance trade-presentation art direction with excellent spacing, clear hierarchy, square-edged layout and a consistent graphical system. This is a static brand board; mockup software is clearly concept only.
DIRECTION 01 — PERIMETER. Palette: security crimson #DC2438, near-black graphite #141B24, white #F7FAFC, steel #7D8B9B. Crimson is prominent on the hero identity field and proposal cover; graphite carries technical specimens and black security workwear. Compact master symbol: two carefully proportioned solid angular perimeter sections interlock optically to create a strong N in their negative space. A square-derived silhouette with squared structural edges, one small controlled opening; an authored precision-machined emblem. No enclosing shield or frame around the whole wordmark, no letter folded into a ribbon. Wordmark medium-bold engineered uppercase NIVRA, upright and readable, custom squared R bowl and consistent N/V/A diagonal angle, NO swoopy joined letters or motorsport slant. Supporting type direction DIN-style industrial sans with readable normal letter spacing. One large crimson hero has white symbol + NIVRA, white smaller SURVEILLANCE INTELLIGENCE, then Before impact. The symbol appears as a crimson embroidery on charcoal uniform and white on a crimson security notice header. Technical graphic motif derives from the two exact perimeter sections. A grounded serious security brand with vivid confident red color, no theatrics.
