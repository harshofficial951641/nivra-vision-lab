Supporting font direction: IBM Plex Sans. This is a proposed pairing; the generated board is not an exact font specimen. Custom logo lettering requires its own master outlines.

Use the numerical contrast pairs in tokens.json. Pairings below 4.5:1 are unsuitable for small text. Do not assume all color combinations or generated labels are accessible.
