# Security-category reference check

Inspected official websites and browser screenshots during this revision. This is a design-category comparison, not a comparison of detection performance.

- Axis: https://www.axis.com/products/network-cameras — black uppercase logo with yellow/red geometric accent; prominent real camera imagery, yellow accents, white surfaces. It clearly foregrounds network-video products. Do not copy its triangle or imply NIVRA manufactures cameras.
- Avigilon: https://www.avigilon.com/ — blue uppercase wordmark, product-family camera/software imagery, white backgrounds and direct security messaging. The logo is restrained; the surrounding products establish the category.
- Verkada: https://www.verkada.com/security-cameras/ — compact dark geometric icon and wordmark, deep blue video-intelligence hero, footage/interface imagery, product-specific sections. Do not copy its check-like symbol or borrow its identity-related feature claims for NIVRA.

Implication for NIVRA: security is conveyed by the whole identity system, especially clear naming, professional lettering and surveillance/operations applications. A conventional shield or literal eye is not necessary. Keep Harsh's strong-color preference while replacing cheerful campus campaigns with security workwear, professional site notices and command-room context.
