# Research-led independent concepts

Built-in Image Gen, no competitor artwork supplied or copied. Same AI-surveillance position across three independently generated executions. Current preference question is optional; precise, technical direction is the working assumption pending a reply.

## 1

Create an ORIGINAL complete identity concept board for NIVRA, an AI SURVEILLANCE AND THREAT-PREVENTION SOFTWARE COMPANY. Landscape 1536x1024. This is for a demanding client who has rejected many meaningless abstract logos and arbitrary palettes. Actual category competitors have been researched: Ambient.ai, Coram, Spot AI, Hakimo and Rhombus. Learn ONLY these principles: Ambient's exacting technical seriousness; Coram's clear visualization of security in real spaces; Spot AI's integration of one graphic idea across identity and video intelligence. DO NOT draw any competitor logo or reproduce its website. No faceted hollow polygon, dot-cluster flower, radial rosette, diamond eye, letter-lock, or similar mark.
BRAND TRUTH: NIVRA processes movement ACROSS A CAMERA SEQUENCE, surfaces a developing threat signal for a human to review, and coordinates response before escalation. The brand should immediately look like advanced AI physical-security technology. It is not cybersecurity, a security-guard agency, a hardware manufacturer, a school campaign or general chatbot.
Non-negotiable: standalone meaningful symbol; plain exact name NIVRA in a normal-proportion medium-weight sans serif, compact spacing. NO extra-wide chunky capitals, letter modifications, N/NV/hidden initials, mascot, animal, latch, fan, mechanical object, generic shield, generic almond eye with pupil, or arbitrary decorative logo. No soft droplet. No sci-fi glow, smoke, haze, artificial spotlight, gradient wash, fake embossed flat logo, pastel lifestyle feel or meaningless luxury adjectives. All flat artwork must be genuinely FLAT, razor-sharp, high-contrast and easy to judge.
FULL BRAND KIT, not logo studies: one dominant flat symbol+name panel, a compact true black/white and reversed proof, a 4-colour role-based palette with exact hex codes, real typography specimen, a coherent brand graphic, one campaign composition and a small product application. Everything uses the SAME symbol. Limit mockups. No business cards, lanyards, animals, guard jackets or office signs. Show a single product-relevant illustrative scene: three consecutive overhead views of two anonymous adult figures approaching the same restricted access point, then one selected evidence moment and a calm human-review panel. It is an ILLUSTRATIVE CONCEPT, not proven detection. No guns, faces, accusations or crime labels. Do not label arbitrary people as threats. Depict no physical confrontation. Security through situational understanding.
Exact text allowed: "NIVRA", "AI surveillance & threat prevention", "Before impact.", "Observe. Understand. Respond.", "Human review required", "Review sequence", "Illustrative concept", the palette and font labels, and the direction label. No made-up claims, metrics, clients, awards, additional slogans or tiny gibberish paragraphs. Main campaign typography and composition should have visual authority. Leave deliberate breathing room; do not fill every inch with panels.
The graphic system must be demonstrably derived from the actual mark, and the mark from the defined observation-to-signal mechanism. Make a polished flat editorial identity board with 5-6 panels of varied scale, not another generic moody grid of mockup photos.
DIRECTION 01: SIGNAL.
Symbol premise: visual observations resolve into one clear signal. Design a compact original SOLID SCAN-PATTERN emblem made from a small number of substantial horizontal bands of UNEQUAL LENGTH, aligned to produce an asymmetrical compact outer silhouette; one deliberately aligned internal break creates a clear continuous negative-space focus through the noisy bands. Think reduction of a video-analysis raster into an identifiable solid mark. Not an E/F/S letter, equalizer, barcode, fingerprint, wifi fan, chevrons, crosshair or shutter. Use maximum five chunky elements with generous gaps, not thin details. It should read as intelligent observation before explanation, not random stripes. Its one-colour version must be visually distinctive.
Palette: Signal blue #1748FF / Obsidian #121724 / Cloud #F3F6FF / Steel #8492A7. Blue has one role: brand recognition and active emphasis; neutral surfaces provide clarity. Do not add lime or a second bright accent. Type: IBM Plex Sans, supporting labels in IBM Plex Mono. Large clean dark masthead with blue symbol and white NIVRA, a big white campaign panel with blue expansive scan-derived horizontal window, and a clear product sequence strip. Blue should feel vivid and deliberate, not vintage Office. The campaign headline "Before impact." must NOT copy Morrow's typography. Label "01 / SIGNAL".

DIRECTION 03: LEAD TIME.
Symbol premise: recognizing a change across time creates an interval to act. Design a compact original icon from THREE STURDY RECTANGULAR TIME-SLICES, translated by small controlled increments, which together form ONE continuous broad negative-space channel with a deliberate interruption in the last slice. The silhouette must be carefully composed and memorable, not a filmstrip, play/pause icon, equalizer, stack-of-documents, letter or three diagonal stripes. Use solid geometric precision and optical balance, slightly eased corners. The time relationship must be visible in the three slices, not merely explained in prose. No bird, fan, gear, flame or eye. Make the sequential channel the distinctive feature.
Palette: Signal amber #FFC146 / Carbon #171D24 / Chalk #F5F5F1 / Alloy #9BA5AF. Confident amber fields balanced by clean chalk and carbon; no safety-chevron tape, workwear imagery or police iconography. Type: IBM Plex Sans. Build the graphic identity through generous temporal intervals and repeated consecutive views, with the decisive frame expanded. Editorial rhythm and technical specificity should carry the AI story. A very large flat amber campaign panel with dark headline, a clean high-contrast flat master logo beside it, narrow film-sequence example and type/palette strip. Label "03 / LEAD TIME".
