# Second exploration — logo review

Status: awaiting user direction. Concept previews, not final logo masters or a completed brand kit.

The actual Mannat Group reference was visually inspected on its official website. Reference relevance is connected geometry, a compact name-related mark, restrained detail and standalone recognisability. NIVRA artwork was generated independently without using Mannat artwork as an edit target.

## Saved and directly inspected

- `concepts-r2/01-continuity.png`: paired curved strokes creating an N. Most rounded and approachable. The rendered construction uses two parts, not the single uninterrupted stroke requested. The stylised barless A is readable in context but should be refined if selected. The attempted correction failed and was not saved as a deliverable.
- `concepts-r2/02-interval.png`: two broad reciprocal curves creating a compact diagonal N. Most solid silhouette and strongest positive/negative relationship. NIVRA lettering has a complete A. Center pinch remains too narrow for final production; a real small-size test and optical separation are necessary after direction selection. Do not describe the current mark as having a reliably open gap.
- `concepts-r2/03-confluence.png`: wide N/V relationship with a rounded lower turn. Simplest and most directional. A tiny white nick in the main turn is an image artifact absent from the small reversed specimen; remove it in any approved vector construction. The N reading is more abstract than in concepts 1 and 2.

All three are black-on-white with small reversed studies. The visible name and tagline are legible at supplied presentation scale. No shields, cameras, eyes, crosshairs, policing imagery or claims of effectiveness appear. Small-size legibility, exact curve consistency, trademark distinctiveness, font licensing and final reproduction remain unverified. Generated specimens are not vector construction evidence.

## Render reliability

All three fresh generations had unusably dark backgrounds despite explicit white-background instructions. Targeted contrast edits produced readable versions. A later small edit to concept 1 returned a fully black result. The last legible version was preserved. Do not use those failed intermediate renders as concept references.
