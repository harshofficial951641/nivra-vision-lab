## Concept 1

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE expertly art-directed, high-resolution landscape brand identity concept board for NIVRA, concept 01 SIGNAL EDITORIAL. This is a static graphic design selection board, not a website screenshot. NIVRA is a calm human-supervised early-intervention technology for public venues: observe a developing sequence, let a human review, coordinate response. It does not identify people or predict crime.

Direction: Swiss editorial force with warm paper, carbon black and a precise vermilion signal. Palette #F3F0E8 #171918 #D4442D. Mostly warm paper; strong black type; limited vermilion. Design an original compact blunt geometric N-inspired mark with one deliberate clean interruption before two strokes meet; thick sturdy forms, no sharp origami, no arrows. Pair with carefully customized, very bold uppercase NIVRA lettering; preserve unmistakable N I V R A. Make the wordmark and mark genuinely memorable and consistent everywhere.

Composition: elegant large landscape agency identity sheet, exact orderly grid, generous margins. Small running header '01 / SIGNAL EDITORIAL' and small 'IDENTITY EXPLORATION'. Top-left dominant black mark plus NIVRA occupying roughly half width, line 'Before impact.' below. Right upper third: large vermilion editorial poster panel with black NIVRA small above oversized stacked exact words 'See it' and 'sooner.' with a deliberate horizontal interval between blocks. Lower band: black-on-paper and reversed white-on-black compact logo studies in two small flat swatches; three big accurately hex-labelled color swatches; a typographic specimen 'Intelligence for the' / 'seconds that matter.' in bold editorial grotesk; a restrained pattern of thick interrupted bars derived exactly from mark. Bottom narrow line 'NIVRA Command / NIVRA Camera / NIVRA Respond'. Include one small 'Human review required' label as a flat graphic specimen, not interface.

Everything is FLAT design, no perspective, no 3D, no desk/mockup staging, no photographic background, no glossy gradients, no fake metrics. Clear typography, excellent composition and professional optical spacing. No shields, eyes, cameras, shutters, birds, crosshairs, police symbols, weapons, faceted cybersecurity shapes, generic tech nodes or faces. No other brands. Only requested short text; avoid paragraph filler and fake certification. One coherent identity, not multiple concepts.

## Concept 3

Use case: logo-brand. Generate ONE highly crafted high-resolution landscape brand identity concept board for NIVRA, concept 03 HUMAN SIGNAL. Calm, human-led technology for noticing developing situations in public venues. Human operators make decisions. Contemporary culture-aware identity, adult, calm and functional; never cute or playful security theatre.

Palette mineral paper #ECEEE7, near black #18201B, vivid acid chartreuse #D6EB45. Create original compact softly squared asymmetric mark: two substantial upright rounded-rectangle forms, one taller, joined by a short offset bridge that stops across a deliberate small open gap; the small pause creates a distinctive compact abstract handoff silhouette. Avoid literal people and hands, avoid hearts, loops, eyes, shields, cameras, smileys, arrows and origami. Looks strong in pure black. Pair with beautifully customized lowercase 'nivra' wordmark, generous but disciplined rounded grotesk, open counter shapes, a characteristic softened cut echoed from mark in the r/v join or terminal. Exact spelling n i v r a, maximum legibility; no childish bubble font.

Composition materially different from editorial and civic boards: an oversized lowercase black 'nivra' lockup dominating the TOP HALF across warm mineral background, tremendous negative space, compact black mark beside it and small line 'Before impact.' below. Small running header '03 / HUMAN SIGNAL' and 'IDENTITY EXPLORATION'. BOTTOM half divided asymmetrically into one broad acid-chartreuse typographic poster occupying left 55 percent with black exact headline 'People make' / 'the call.' and small restrained NIVRA wordmark; on right a clean miniature brand manual region with black and reversed-white mark studies, three large HEX-labelled swatches and a simple type sample 'Quiet until it matters.' Derived background motif: sparse enlarged softly squared separated shapes, not decorative doodles. Small footer 'NIVRA Command / NIVRA Camera / NIVRA Respond'. Include 'Human review required' in dark legible type on mineral, no white text on chartreuse.

Keep sophisticated contemporary brand-studio finish, optical typography, flat graphic design, crisp outlines. No photos, 3D, devices, web UI, generic cards, shadows, gradients, fake endorsements, measurements or product claims. Clearly one identity system, no alternative logos on same sheet except exact same mark in monochrome/reverse.
