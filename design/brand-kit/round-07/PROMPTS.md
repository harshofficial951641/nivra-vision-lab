## A

Use the attached NIVRA surveillance/security brand board as the fixed presentation reference. The client says this first direction is okayish but the angular interlocking N logo is very generic. Keep the crimson #DC2438, graphite #141B24, white #F7FAFC and steel #7D8B9B palette, serious SECURITY COMPANY identity, uppercase name NIVRA, exact descriptor SURVEILLANCE INTELLIGENCE, and exact line Before impact. Keep the same high-quality coherent full-kit presentation: large crimson logo hero, flat monochrome logo proof, palette, typography, monitored-site notice beside generic unbranded camera, embroidered security uniform, control room wall, responder phone and technical proposal. We are evaluating NEW LOGO IDEAS, not new palettes or consumer aesthetics.
REPLACE THE ORIGINAL ANGULAR N EVERYWHERE. The result must be visibly and substantially different from the source logo in silhouette and construction. Don't simply round or rearrange its two pieces. Use the same new master logo identically on all applications. Keep readable spelling NIVRA and clean optical letterspacing. Refine the wordmark to work with your new mark rather than pairing it with anonymous stock uppercase type. Strong, purposeful, quietly distinctive. The audience is security directors, control-room operators and security teams. No sports, esports, medical, charity, fashion, construction-company, finance or generic VPN logo.
The main hero and monochrome proof show sharp flat vector-like logo shapes without texture, perspective or glow. Photographic mockups remain secondary. Every pixel fully opaque, no transparency. Do not add TM/registration marks, awards, fake statistics or certification. No shields, eye shapes, shutters, lens apertures, birds, police symbols, weapons, crosshairs, AI sparkles, generic three-stripe chevrons, hexagons, origami folds, infinity loops or padlock icons. Preserve small CONCEPT labels on applications. Product uses existing cameras and human-reviewed alerts, not facial identification or crime prediction. Sign: VIDEO MONITORING / Human-reviewed alerts. Phone: NIVRA Respond / Response requested / Acknowledge. No invented additional slogans.
Make a NEW complete board of the same format, same palette and consistent comparable application scale.
VARIANT A — OWN THE NAME. Eliminate the detached symbol from the main identity completely: the custom-drawn uppercase NIVRA wordmark itself is the primary logo. Invent polished proprietary-looking letterforms with a distinctive central IV relationship: the I is a well-defined upright, the adjacent V has a subtly squared low vertex and a deliberate short gap to the R. The rounded-square R bowl and the A crossbar share a disciplined horizontal height, giving the name a recognizable internal rhythm. Include one surprising but understated functional cut in a letter counter, not repeated stencil slashes on every letter. Correct conventional A crossbar, all five letters immediately readable; medium-bold upright proportions, not italic sports/racing or aggressive sci-fi. Spend the craft on negative space, kerning, balance, and one memorable letter junction. NO DEFAULT GEOMETRIC N ICON beside the name. For the compact phone/app treatment, use a small IV ligature directly extracted from the custom wordmark. Main hero is large white NIVRA on crimson, descriptor below. Show the full custom wordmark in monochrome in the proof panel. The logo should read as an established specialist surveillance company with an unmistakably authored name. Avoid an extra enormous tagline crowding the wordmark.
