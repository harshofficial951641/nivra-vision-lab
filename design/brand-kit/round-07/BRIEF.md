# Refining the crimson security direction

Harsh: the first round-06 concept is okayish, but its logo is too generic and should be different in a good way. This is permission to refine, not approval of a final mark.

Hold crimson/graphite, surveillance/security tone and application context as a working baseline. Explore three genuinely different logo approaches using the same reference board: A custom full wordmark with an IV signature; B abstract Intercept symbol; C single crafted NV ligature. Do not repaint/rearrange the old angular interlocking N.

Primary assessment: readable NIVRA, distinct silhouette or letter signature in one color, security-category fit, consistency across applications. Avoid relying on copy explanations or polished mockups to excuse a weak logo. Compare alternatives with a constant palette.

Universal router and existing research are retained; no app implementation is in scope. Exactly three variants; user selection before final vectors and full production exports.
