# Logo refinement review

Three independently generated alternatives using the same inspected reference board. All were directly viewed. Palette and security context stay constant to focus the comparison on the logo.

## A: custom wordmark

The name is the primary identity; the original icon is removed. The open R counter is the most identifiable letter feature. The secondary IV treatment can read as a Roman numeral, so its compact-icon role is not resolved. Wordmark width and sport-like cues deserve attention if selected.

## B: Intercept

The strongest departure from the repeated N-monogram approach. An asymmetric return and a separate short upright create a compact silhouette. It may also read as a J-like or script-like glyph; this is a shape association, not an asserted linguistic meaning. The mark's relationship to the NIVRA name depends on consistent use. No originality or trademark search conclusion is implied.

The initial generated wordmark used a barless A; a targeted correction requested a conventional crossbar while preserving the abstract symbol. The corrected board was directly inspected: the hero and proof A now have a crossbar; mark and color direction are retained.

## C: curved N study

Although requested as an NV ligature, the output reads primarily as a curved N. It is more conventional and does not sufficiently answer the distinctiveness request. Not recommended for further refinement. Included as an honestly evaluated alternative, not described as a successful final identity.

## Common boundaries

All boards are raster exploration. Final consistent glyphs, master curves, icon design, small-size and embroidery proofs, exact licensed supporting font and production exports remain pending a direction choice. The same colors were reused; relevant numerical contrast evidence remains in tokens.json. Photos and screens are generated concept applications.
