## Visual findings

- 01: clear readable nivra, integrated eye above i, confident colour and a simpler hierarchy than earlier rounds. The large dimensional eye is a campaign interpretation, not a separate logo master. The literal almond eye remains familiar; no originality or trademark claim is made. Corrected cover to Before impact. Final phone uses white text on dark ink.
- 02: custom shaped lettering, vertical two-part aperture, orchid contrast against white. Added the missing i dot. Corrected cover and dark button label. The compact cover wordmark remains an illustrative raster specimen. The symbol can read as a leaf/seed; its eye reading is less immediate.
- 03: readable wordmark, connected upper eyelid/pupil and short baseline, clear dark/light system. Corrected detached pupil in enlarged motif, cover line and phone description. Raster curvature differs slightly between the main and enlarged specimens; final vector construction must use one actual master.

The reference's simplicity is reflected in sparse identity-led compositions. No Morrow mascot or wordmark was reused. Exact geometry is not approved or production-ready. Supporting fonts are proposals, not claims about the generated lettering.

## Checks

Image files are verified as readable, at least 1500 x 1000 and fully opaque by build_package.py. Intended hex pairs are calculated in tokens.json; ink/surface, reversed ink/surface and the separate pure-black on-primary token meet 4.5:1. Open Iris carbon on orchid failed at 4.40:1; pure black is specified for small text on orchid. The mockup does not prove exact pixel contrast. These are palette checks, not a claim that every rendered pixel or tiny mockup label is accessible. ZIP contents and CRC are checked by package_concepts.py. validation.json records dimensions and file hashes.

No native UI was implemented; no browser, interaction or production deployment claims apply. Final identity assets, templates, font licensing and PDF guide follow selection under the user's explicit three-concept gate.
