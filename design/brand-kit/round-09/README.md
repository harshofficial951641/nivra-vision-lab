# NIVRA — three reference-led identity concepts

These are alternatives for selection, not an approved final brand kit.

1. **Apricot Aperture** — bold lowercase lettering with an eye replacing the i's dot; persimmon, apricot, near-black and porcelain.
2. **Open Iris** — shaped lettering and a two-part vertical eye aperture; orchid, lavender, carbon and white.
3. **Clear View** — connected eyelid/pupil gesture, rounded lettering and a dark field; turquoise, mint, black-green and chalk.

Each directory contains a full-resolution brand-board.png, GUIDELINES.md, tokens.json and tokens.css. The visual boards show logo direction, palette and illustrative applications. The guideline files give proposed supporting typography and practical colour use. Brand tokens record exact intended hex values and calculated contrast pairs.

Morrow was supplied by Harsh as an aesthetic reference. Its screenshot is retained locally for project context but is excluded from the delivery archive. No Morrow asset is offered as part of NIVRA's identity.

## After selection

Refine the chosen logo into actual vector master geometry; verify small sizes and one-colour use; export transparent PNG families; finalize supporting fonts and licences; create editable templates and the final PDF brand guide. No direction, palette or generated typeface is approved yet.
