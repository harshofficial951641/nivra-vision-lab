# Round 05 independent generation prompts

## 1

Design one complete, visually striking brand identity concept board for NIVRA. Landscape 3:2, high resolution, FULLY OPAQUE every pixel, zero transparent background. NIVRA is a human-supervised early-intervention system for campuses and public spaces; its product line is exactly "Before impact." Feeling: alert, capable, contemporary, confident, human. The client has rejected FOUR rounds because their logos feel generic and the boards feel bland, neutral and colorless. This time saturated color IS the identity: at least two thirds of the composition uses large, rich flat color surfaces, not grey/white panels with tiny accent stripes. It must look like a designed brand with character, not a conservative software or security company. Bring real typographic and symbol craft. Distinctive without childishness. Do not rely on a paragraph to justify the logo.
Deliver an art-directed FULL VISUAL BRAND KIT with: a dominant primary logo panel, a standalone symbol and one-color logo study, four clearly labelled exact HEX palette swatches, a short expressive type specimen, a bold staff credential/lanyard, a public-space sign, an app icon and a responder phone example, and a branded presentation cover or graphic pattern. Compose these in an engaging asymmetrical editorial board with varied scale and clean edges; not a dashboard/card UI. The exact same master logo must be reused in every application. Keep word NIVRA correctly spelled. Design custom logo lettering, not a generic font typed out. For supporting text choose a readable contemporary sans. Display exact product line "Before impact." and optional sample "Notice. Review. Respond." No other marketing slogans. Phone text only "NIVRA Respond", "Response requested", "Acknowledge". Small label "CONCEPT" on applications. No fake metrics, affiliations, deployment evidence or QR codes.
Public-space scene calm ordinary contemporary campus daylight, no threat imagery. No shields, eyes, surveillance lenses, camera shutters, police emblems, birds, weapons, crosshairs, AI sparkles, recycling symbols, generic swirly app icons, sharp folded origami, infinity loops or disconnected decorative blobs. NO trademarks/TM/registration marks. Exact flat color in identity panels; photographic lighting only in application panels. The logo must be compelling in solid black too, with no dependency on color tricks. Do not copy an existing company's logo.
CONCEPT 01: VIOLET / ACID LIME. Primary electric violet #6027F5, vivid lime #D9FF43, near-black #171220 and clear white #FCFCFF. HUGE violet field with a lime identity dominates; a second lime field uses dark purple lettering. No dusty lavender, muted aubergine, haze or neon glow. Create a distinctive tightly drawn lowercase "nivra" wordmark with energetic, substantial custom lettering: sharp shoulder of n, clear i dot, a broad low v that creates a recognizable valley, and a confident r/a pair. Restrained curve tension and slightly compressed proportions; avoid generic bubbly geometric sans. Give it a memorable companion NV ligature, authored as a single compact solid glyph with two structural verticals and one flowing diagonal, a distinct clean white opening, no ribbon, no lightning bolt; same angle/curve language as the wordmark. Let the designer resolve the exact form rather than explaining literal symbols. Wordmark leads. Supporting type direction Space Grotesk. Main large logo upper left, diagonal scale shifts in a graphic motif extracted from actual letter shapes, generous lime staff badge with violet logo, saturated purple public notice with large lime typography, phone/interface in sensible light background, lime app tile with violet mark. Bold, refined, slightly unexpected. Strong confident clean typography, no giant spaced-out tagline.

CONCEPT 02: SCARLET / ULTRAMARINE. Intense scarlet #F13B34, saturated ultramarine #283EE8, paper white #FFFFFF, ink #181820. A rich RED large hero panel with WHITE custom uppercase NIVRA is dominant. CONCEPT 03: TEAL / TANGERINE / DEEP PURPLE. Saturated teal #008E88, vivid tangerine #FF7B35, deep purple #3D1768, pale mint #E5FFF6. Dominant TEAL and ORANGE color fields across the whole board, purple as structured typography and compact dark background. Human, expressive, confident public-space technology, not a wellness brand, NGO or children's product. Invent a sculpted memorable title-case "Nivra" logotype, medium-bold with real bespoke character: a strong capital N whose diagonal joins have one rounded internal return, a distinctive open lowercase v, exceptionally balanced r and a, normal i dot. Create a matching single sculpted N compact icon that is exactly the capital N of the logotype, not a separate ornamental emblem. Develop a bold graphic pattern by cropping and repeating the N's characteristic curve and open notch, not adding unrelated blobs. Contrast large color panels and crisp smaller specimens; make the full board feel like an identity people would actually remember. Exact line Before impact. No sepia, terracotta, beige or pale apricot.
