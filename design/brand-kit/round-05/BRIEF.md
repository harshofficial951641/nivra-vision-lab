# Round 05: color carries the identity

Status: exploration. Harsh rejected round 04 as bland and colorless, and disliked its palettes. Harsh said ok after being offered three bolder color territories, without selecting one; explore all three rather than treating one as approved.

Three independently generated full visual kits: violet/lime, scarlet/ultramarine, teal/tangerine/purple. Color must occupy substantial surfaces, not token accent lines on grey. Each has original name-led logo lettering, monochrome evidence, palette, typography direction and representative applications.

Keep the brand brief: human-supervised early intervention, calm capability, contemporary public-space identity. No security cliches or invented product claims. Full saturated presentation does not imply alarm colors throughout the actual operational interface.

Lead remains logo-design, support brandkit and built-in Image Gen. Upstream universal router retained; static brand concepts do not invoke an app implementation route. Existing official-site competitor research is reused. Exactly three visual alternatives; final production follows Harsh's selection.
