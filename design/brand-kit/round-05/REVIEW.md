# Round 05 review

The three full boards were generated independently and inspected directly. All use saturated color on large surfaces and give the name more substantial typographic character. Each includes a readable NIVRA/Nivra/nivra identity and a monochrome study. This documents visual inspection, not Harsh's acceptance or evidence of trademark distinctiveness.

- Electric: lime on violet dominates, with a joined NV companion symbol. Lowercase a and the vr junction have the most unusual forms and need small-size legibility tests if selected.
- Signal: strongest uppercase presence; the R/A connection creates an authored silhouette but the weight and sport-like energy may need softening. A nonsensical generated supporting headline was corrected to Notice. Review. Respond. and the revised board was visually checked.
- Vivid: teal and orange dominate with purple type; the N is repeated as a pattern. More rounded and approachable than the other two. Small light type on teal needs contrast-aware refinement; the tokens report actual intended pairings.

Incidental slogans and fictional credential names in the generated scenes are not approved copy or real people/product evidence. Board font specimens are approximate, and color gradients introduced by generation are not master color definitions. The final identity must use the exact tokens and vector masters established after selection.

Final SVG/PNG logo families, optical-size proofs, exact licensed supporting fonts, production application templates and PDF guide remain pending selection. These boards must not be represented as final production artwork.
