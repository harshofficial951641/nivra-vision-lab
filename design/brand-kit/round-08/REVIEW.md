# Eye identity review

Three independently generated full visual kits were directly inspected. Each includes an eye mark, readable NIVRA spelling, a one-color proof, a new palette and surveillance/security applications. None reuses crimson/graphite. User selection is pending.

## 01 N-Sight

The N inside the iris gives the clearest connection between the symbol and the name. Openings at the outer edges avoid a fully closed almond outline. The inner N requires optical refinement and a small-size proof; small illustrative cover text is not production typography.

## 02 Watchpoint

Asymmetric upper and lower arcs plus an off-center pupil create an open-eye expression. It is simpler than 01 but closer to familiar eye iconography and could appear more casual. The green/citron scheme needs comparison with existing technology identities before finalization; no originality clearance is claimed.

## 03 Sightline

The iris and outer eye connect through a horizontal interval. This is the clearest structural feature apart from a hidden letter. Amber and ink provide strong visible contrast and a distinct security-signage application. The narrow gap and tapered tips need small-size and embroidery tests if selected.

## Evidence and limitations

Exact intended colors and numerical contrast pairs accompany the boards. They do not establish that every generated raster label is compliant. Typeface specimens are illustrative. Images of cameras, offices, uniforms and software are generated concepts; hardware is generic existing infrastructure.

No basis was found to claim the name translates as sight. The visual direction is grounded in the user's request and the product function. See BRIEF.md for dictionary source links and scope of the check.

Final SVG construction, font selection/licensing, consistent optical details, icon/small-size variants, transparent assets, production templates, print proof and final PDF guide remain pending selection.
