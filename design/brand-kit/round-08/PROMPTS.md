# Independent eye-logo prompts

## 1

Create a complete professional full-color brand identity concept board for NIVRA, a SURVEILLANCE AND SECURITY TECHNOLOGY COMPANY. Client specifically requests an EYE-BASED LOGO, replacing previous generic N monograms. The eye must communicate sight, vigilance, observed signals and human-reviewed surveillance. Make it distinctive and appropriate for security operations; not an optician/eye hospital, cosmetics, mystical/religious all-seeing eye, television network, AI robot, cartoon or sports brand. NO assertion about the linguistic meaning of NIVRA: unverified. Product line "Before impact." Descriptor "SURVEILLANCE INTELLIGENCE". Exact name NIVRA.
The crimson/graphite palette has been explicitly REJECTED. Use the new colors below, confidently on large surfaces; no predominantly grey board with tiny accent lines. Preserve authoritative surveillance category through precise lettering, operations applications, secure-premises notices and command-room imagery. No cheerful campus marketing or community slogans. Keep the eye clear, authored, compact, strong at small size. Design the silhouette and negative space, not just a stock almond outline around a dot. It must work in one solid color without glow or tiny decorative lines. Avoid copying CBS, NVIDIA, Hikvision, Securitas or any other recognizable logo. No shield/lock/camera-shutter/weapon/police insignia/triangle enclosing eye.
High-resolution landscape 3:2 FULL VISUAL BRAND KIT, entire rectangular image fully opaque, no transparent pixels. Main logo is the biggest visual. Include a strong hero identity panel, a large flat black one-color proof, 4 labelled exact HEX palette swatches, supporting typography direction specimen, a professionally mounted VIDEO MONITORING notice beside an ordinary UNBRANDED existing security camera, a security-workwear embroidery mockup, a believable compact command-room/workstation scene marked CONCEPT, a responder phone marked CONCEPT with NIVRA Respond / Response requested / Acknowledge, and a technical proposal cover. Every application repeats the SAME new eye symbol and wordmark identically. Existing camera hardware not branded NIVRA; it is an integration context, no manufacturing claim.
Wordmark: custom confident uppercase NIVRA, clean medium-bold engineered lettering, upright stance, normal readable A crossbar, carefully kerned. Avoid generic exaggerated block letters, stencils and italic racing fonts. Display "Before impact." with normal spacing and sensible scale. The ONLY supporting phrases allowed are "SURVEILLANCE INTELLIGENCE", "VIDEO MONITORING", "Human-reviewed alerts", "Observe. Review. Respond.", "TECHNICAL PROPOSAL", "CONCEPT", font labels and palette labels. No other invented slogans or promises, no TM/registration, awards, fake metrics, certification or QR. No visible identified-person boxes, crime prediction, suspect imagery or threats.
Art direction: premium sharp graphic design, clean structured composition, varied scale, confident color. Flat uniform color in logo specimens, natural photography in applications, no logo gradients, chrome, glow or smoke. One-color proof must be easy to examine. Do not output an actual website.
CONCEPT 01: N-SIGHT. Eye construction: two substantial softly squared curved lids define a horizontally stable eye; the iris is a compact solid vertical form containing a crisp NEGATIVE-SPACE N. The N is a clever second reading; the first read is an alert, neutral eye. Make it one integrated authored emblem, not a generic eye with a letter pasted inside. A small intentional break on one outer side of the eye makes a recognizable silhouette. Use one common line weight, open enough spaces and precisely controlled radii. No lashes, realistic anatomy, tear drop or aggressive eyebrow. Pair with elegant precise NIVRA lettering sharing squared inside corners. Color: vivid iris indigo #4935D4, deep blue ink #172344, clear sky #75D5F0, frost #F1F6FF. Indigo is a large vivid hero field with white identity; sky supports the pattern/secondary panel. Black monochrome proof on frost. Supporting type direction Inter Tight. Indigo public monitoring sign and white embroidery on indigo-dark workwear. Technical cover predominantly indigo/sky with a cropped eye curve motif; operational phone on a readable light surface. Mature optical intelligence and security.
