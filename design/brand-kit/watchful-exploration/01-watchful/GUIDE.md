## Typography

Manrope: regular 400 for body, semibold 600 for headings and supporting NIVRA text, bold 700 sparingly. Body 16 px or larger at 1.5 line height. Keep lettering upright, normal-width and readable. Font files and SIL Open Font License supplied. Generated board lettering is illustrative; these font files define the reproducible type system.
