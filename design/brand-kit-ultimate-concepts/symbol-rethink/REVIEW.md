# NIVRA symbol rethink

User rejected the selection of B1 and requested a clearer eye plus other concepts. B1 and its colour treatments are not approved. This round explores 12 rough constructions across eye, warning bell and protective hand subjects using three independent built-in imagegen generations. A fourth imagegen call placed the transparent eye/hand images onto a legible white comparison sheet. All delivered images visually inspected.

## Assessment

- E1: clearest eye candidate to refine. Open counter and separate pupil solve B1's hood-like reading. Tapering and small outer gap still require refinement; not sufficiently distinctive yet.
- E2: eye reads clearly but arbitrary cut adds little and feels like an icon variation.
- E3: reject as a standard visibility icon.
- E4: pointed symmetry feels cosmetic and decorative.
- W1/W3/W4: recognizable but too close to stock bell symbols; do not advance as drawn.
- W2: only alternate worth testing further. Bell mouth and clapper create an eye reading, giving a visible double meaning of warning and observation. Still too literal and stock-like in the hanging loop and bell silhouette. This is a hypothesis for refinement, not an approved logo.
- H1/H2: too illustrative and charity/care-like; H2 has awkward anatomy and negative space.
- H3/H4: simplified but still generic helping-hand symbols. Reject hand direction for current company positioning.

These are subject/construction studies, not three finished identity concepts. No vector masters, font selection, small-size proof, trademark assessment or final kit delivered. No final direction approved.

## Prompts

### Study 1

Design four original logo sketches for NIVRA, a human-supervised early-warning security company. Simple pure BLACK symbols on an OPAQUE WHITE sheet, 2 by 2 grid. Labels only E1 E2 E3 E4. Explore a clearly recognizable OPEN HUMAN EYE. The previous attempt failed because a big black upper mass resembled a hood and squashed the white eye. Correct this with a generously open almond-shaped counter, upper and lower lids of balanced weight, and a round pupil visibly separated from the lids. Every sketch should read immediately as an eye, never a helmet, letter, cap or spiral. Create four distinctly drawn constructions, not copies: E1 a calm frontal eye with a subtle asymmetric opening at one outer corner; E2 a slightly upright compact eye with an offset pupil and a single deliberate interruption in its outline; E3 an economical continuous contour with a generous pupil separated from the contour; E4 a softer bold eye silhouette with large clean white space, no heavy canopy. Emphasis on optical quality, memorable proportions and restraint. Not stock UI icons: explore silhouette and positive-negative balance, while staying immediately recognizable. NO eyelashes, eyebrow, tear, lashes, angry angles, Egyptian symbolism, rings, lens, shutter, shields, letters, wordmarks, decorative cuts, colour or mockups. Four large flat marks with ample space.

### Study 2

Four original emblem sketches for NIVRA on an OPAQUE WHITE sheet, flat BLACK, 2 by 2 grid, labels only W1 W2 W3 W4. Subject: EARLY-WARNING BELL. A familiar simple bell silhouette, pared down and memorable like a carefully designed brand emblem, not an app notification icon. The bell's distinctiveness should come from proportions and one coherent structural choice, not random decoration. W1 a softly asymmetric bell with a gently offset hanging clapper integrated into the silhouette; W2 a compact bell whose white internal opening is shaped by an elegantly curved lip; W3 a side-view bell with one generous diagonal open mouth and a simple clapper; W4 a nearly square soft bell with a distinct flared base and single off-centre white slit. Bell must remain recognizable, simple enough to sketch from memory, at most three solid shapes, no hairlines. Clean considered curves and believable bell form. No ringing lines, notification badges, numbers, exclamation marks, smiley face, alarm clock, tower, beacon, shields, letters, eyes, gradients, 3D, textures, shadows, brand names or mockups. Four independent construction sketches, not final presentation.

### Study 3

Four original logo emblem sketches, OPAQUE WHITE sheet and flat BLACK ink, 2 by 2 grid, labels only H1 H2 H3 H4. Brand NIVRA: a human-supervised early-intervention system; humans take protective action. Subject: a gently CUPPED PROTECTIVE HAND, recognizable as a hand rather than abstract ribbons. Explore economical strong silhouettes: H1 side-on hand curving up, four fingers grouped as a single clean mass with just one small division and a clear thumb; H2 three-quarter cupped palm creating a distinct open white hollow; H3 two reduced curved fingers and a thumb forming one coherent protective gesture; H4 a compact side-on hand with a generous thumb opening, smoothly flowing wrist, asymmetrical rounded contour. Should feel calm, capable, protective, not sentimental or aggressive. Only the hand itself, no person, heart, globe, leaf, eye or object sitting in it. Avoid raised stop palm, fist, handshake, charity-logo composition, stock holding-hand-under-object pictogram, letters, monograms, impossible extra fingers, thin anatomical detail, arrows, gradients, colour, mockups, typography or slogans. Four notably different silhouette designs with crisp curves and ample white space. Simple meaningful recognizable subjects, no forced symbolism.

Repair prompt: combine existing E1-E4 and H1-H4 into two labelled rows on an opaque white sheet, preserving black mark geometry. Minor generated shape drift remains possible.
