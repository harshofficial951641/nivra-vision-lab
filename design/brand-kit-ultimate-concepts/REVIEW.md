Three independent raster boards generated and visually inspected. Source: NIVRA_COMPLETE_CONTEXT_FOR_HARSH_JAIN.md and brand-kit/BRIEF.md, with prior rejection notes consulted. No prior identity treated as approved. Existing work preserved.

Status: awaiting direction selection, not a completed production kit.

- Trace: consistent repeated mark, readable main name and category. Orange gives energy, but tapered forms and dot suggest a shuttlecock; not recommended as a final symbol. Generated NIVRA Sans is not a real selected font. Extra small campaign copy is unapproved.
- Parallax: strongest composition and most coherent product-family application. Symbol repeats consistently in monochrome and reversed treatments. The two-plane mark is generic and potentially letter-like; requires refinement before final approval. Preferred overall system, not an approved logo.
- Persistence: coherent palette and applications; waveform reads more like audio than visual security. Hairline incision is vulnerable at small sizes. Generated alphabet specimen is malformed. Not recommended as a final symbol.

All imagery is conceptual, not deployment evidence. Generated gradients and printed colour labels do not establish exact output colour. Additional slogans are provisional, not accepted brand copy.
