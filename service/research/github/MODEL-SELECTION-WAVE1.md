Frozen before any round3 milestone development predictions or reserved-audit predictions. Training loss is already observable and is explicitly excluded from selection. Candidates are baseline X3D-M and the completed continuous run's epochs 3, 10 and 20; threshold remains strictly greater than 0.5. No additional fitting or threshold search follows this comparison.

Use only the four existing development sources and densely reviewed online development normal groups. Keep all reserved-audit scores unopened until one model and this policy are frozen. Online wave1 currently supplies no development contact-proxy source; report that limitation. Neither older development sports footage nor new sports/drill proxies establish hostile-fight accuracy.

## Rule

1. Require exactly paired source-time windows for every compared model. Exclude unknown windows from label-based summaries. Ordinary-motion windows require full explicit normal coverage; contact-proxy windows require at least half their actual span covered by positive proxy annotations. No onset tolerance or retrospective event credit.
2. The original grappling source is the eligible development contact control. For a milestone to qualify, its flagged compatible-window count must be at least baseline on that source. The kick source is a short-action sensitivity control: require no decrease in its raw flagged-window count, but do not call those windows eligible positives or count them as event recall. Its two annotated episodes total only 1.6 seconds and cannot cover half of a roughly four-second window. The dependent cropped grappling view is also a separately reported sensitivity check and does not get another source vote.
3. Require no increase over baseline in flagged fully annotated normal windows on any newly admitted development normal source. Separately require no increase in raw flags on the historical boxing and squat negative-context controls. Their original corpus has empty event arrays, so those historical context controls do not supply explicit normal-interval denominators. Compare exactly paired windows on each source. Unknown and short noneligible spans cannot become normal.
4. Among qualifying models, minimize the equally weighted source-group mean normal-window flag fraction. Then maximize the eligible grappling contact-window flag fraction. This is only one contact source group. Break exact ties in favor of less adaptation: baseline, epoch 3, epoch 10, epoch 20. Missing or unpaired source evidence blocks selection rather than being imputed.
5. A milestone must strictly improve one of those development summaries to displace baseline. If none does, retain baseline and report that additional training did not demonstrate a useful development upgrade. Reduced training loss or higher confidence alone does not count as an improvement.

After selection, save exact candidate/artifact/run-seal/corpus/policy hashes, then run the selected model and unchanged baseline on reserved-audit groups once. Reserved results do not feed back into fitting, thresholds or milestone choice. If later used for tuning, mark them development and obtain a new audit.
